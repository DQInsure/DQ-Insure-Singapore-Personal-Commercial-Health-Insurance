For your business

Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA)Required by law
What it covers
If an employee is injured or falls ill because of work, it covers their medical bills and medical leave wages. It also pays compensation for permanent incapacity or death.
When you need it
Required under WICA for all manual workers and for non-manual employees earning S$2,600 or less a month.
If you don't
Fine up to $10,000, jail up to 12 months, or both.
Get a quote →
Foreign Worker Medical Insurance (FWMI)Required by law
What it covers
Helps pay your workers' hospital and day surgery bills, including for illnesses unrelated to work.
When you need it
Required for every Work Permit and S Pass holder, with at least S$60,000 of cover per worker per year.
If you don't
Fine up to $10,000, and you can be barred from hiring foreign workers.
Get a quote →
Foreign Worker BondRequired by law
What it covers
A guarantee to MOM that you and your worker will follow the Work Permit conditions. MOM may claim against the bond if a condition is breached.
When you need it
A S$5,000 bond is required for each non-Malaysian Work Permit holder before they arrive in Singapore.
If you don't
You can lose the full $5,000 if the worker goes missing or salary is paid late.
Get a quote →
Motor FleetRequired by law
What it covers
Covers several company vehicles under one policy. Depending on the plan, it can also cover damage to your own vehicles.
When you need it
Useful if your business operates several vehicles. Each vehicle driven on Singapore roads must be insured for injury or death caused to others.
If you don't
Fine up to $1,000, jail up to 3 months, or both.

Contractor All RiskOften required
What it covers
Covers accidental damage to construction or renovation work and materials on site. It can also cover claims if the work injures someone or damages their property.
When you need it
Useful for contractors carrying out building or renovation work. Your client may require cover before work starts.
Without it
One site accident can cost you the project and the third-party claims.

Public LiabilityOften required
What it covers
Covers claims if your business is legally responsible for injuring someone or damaging their property.
When you need it
Useful if customers visit your premises or your team works at a client's site.
Without it
Legal costs and compensation come out of your pocket.
